## Context

During my professional training as a coach and public transport driver, the material to master covered very different areas: social regulations, urban and intercity rules, accessibility, professional calculations, onboard documents and tachograph rules.

CTCR Trainer started from a simple observation: **I needed a tool I would genuinely want to use for revision**.

## The problem

A large part of the curriculum depends on precise rules, exceptions, durations and calculations. Reading a course again does not always prove that you can apply those rules to a concrete situation.

The product therefore needed to make it easy to switch between **understanding, memorising and practising**.

## The solution

CTCR Trainer brings lessons and training together in an interface designed for daily use, especially on mobile.

It includes training and exam quiz modes, shuffled questions and answers, timers, retrying mistakes and dedicated professional calculation content.

## A project born from a real need

CTCR Trainer directly connects my two professional paths. It did not start from an abstract idea about digital learning; it answers a real need encountered during a professional training programme.

That origin also explains my interest in learning tools, mobility and software designed for field work.
